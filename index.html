## ETH Blockchain Club — Website Designer Prompt

Use the following design tokens when building UI for the ETH Blockchain Club.

---

### Colors

#### Backgrounds & Surfaces
- Background:       #04060f  (rgb 4, 6, 15)
- Surface:          #080c18  (rgb 8, 12, 24)
- Cards:            #0d1424  (rgb 13, 20, 36)
- Elevated surface: #121b30  (rgb 18, 27, 48)

#### Accents & Highlights
- Accent:           #5b7eff  (rgb 91, 126, 255)
- Secondary accent: #a78bfa  (rgb 167, 139, 250)
- Highlight:        #2eb9ff  (rgb 46, 185, 255)
- Buttons:          #16539b  (rgb 22, 83, 155)

#### Text
- Primary text:     #eef2ff  (rgb 238, 242, 255)
- Body text:        #7086b8  (rgb 112, 134, 184)
- Muted text:       #6b7fa8  (rgb 107, 127, 168)
- Dim text:         #2e3a52  (rgb 46, 58, 82)

#### Borders
- Border:           rgba(99, 130, 255, 0.10)
- Strong border:    rgba(99, 130, 255, 0.22)

#### Logo Colors
- Logo left/right:  #16539b
- Logo center:      #2eb9ff

---

### Typography

#### Typefaces
- Titles/headings:  Montserrat
- Body/UI text:     IBM Plex Sans
- Code/labels:      Geist Mono

#### Font Sizes
- Display:          clamp(48px, 6vw, 80px)
- Hero:             clamp(34px, 4.4vw, 62px)
- Section heading:  clamp(24px, 3vw, 40px)
- Subsection:       20px
- Card title:       18px
- Body:             16px
- Small text:       14px
- Label:            12px
- Footer/meta:      11px
- Tiny label:       10px

#### Font Weights
- 300 — body light
- 400 — body
- 500 — links / buttons
- 600 — subheads
- 700 — headings
- 800 — hero / display

---

### Buttons

- Primary bg:       #16539b
- Primary hover:    #2eb9ff
- Outline hover bg: #2eb9ff
- Social pill bg:   rgba(22, 83, 155, 0.12)
- Social pill border: rgba(22, 83, 155, 0.22)
- Padding:          10px 22px
- Font weight:      500

---

### Spacing

- Navbar height:              60px
- Hero gap:                   64px
- What-we-do section padding: 84px 3rem
- Hero spacing:               42px 3rem 32px
- Pillar card padding:        20px 18px
- Button padding:             10px 22px
- Footer pill padding:        8px 14px
- Navbar horizontal padding:  2.5rem
- Section horizontal padding: 3rem

## ETH Blockchain Club — General Purpose Designer Prompt

Use only the typography and color tokens below when creating non-website assets (banners, slides, social graphics, posters, etc.).

----

### Colors

#### Backgrounds & Surfaces
- Background:       #04060f  (rgb 4, 6, 15)
- Surface:          #080c18  (rgb 8, 12, 24)
- Cards:            #0d1424  (rgb 13, 20, 36)
- Elevated surface: #121b30  (rgb 18, 27, 48)

#### Accents & Highlights
- Accent:           #5b7eff  (rgb 91, 126, 255)
- Secondary accent: #a78bfa  (rgb 167, 139, 250)
- Highlight:        #2eb9ff  (rgb 46, 185, 255)
- Buttons:          #16539b  (rgb 22, 83, 155)

#### Text
- Primary text:     #eef2ff  (rgb 238, 242, 255)

- Muted text:       #6b7fa8  (rgb 107, 127, 168)
